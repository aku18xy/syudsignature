Section 04

High-Reliability Architecture & Offline Resilience

IndexedDB + LAN Sockets
STEP 01
Order Punched on POS

Waiter inputs order. Saved immediately to client-side IndexedDB.

STEP 02
Direct LAN ESC/POS Print

Raw ESC/POS bytes fired directly to local 80mm kitchen printer over Wi-Fi.

STEP 03
Online / Offline Branch

If online, pushes to Vercel API. If disconnected, queues locally.

STEP 04
Background Cloud Sync

Background worker flushes queue into PostgreSQL upon reconnect.

ESC/POS Thermal Printing & Cash Drawer

Raw text and ESC/POS hex commands sent via Port 9100 TCP Socket or WebUSB. Solenoid RJ11 kick opens cash drawer on cash tenders.

Sub-Second KDS WebSocket Bus

Scoped channel per outlet (`outlet:{id}:kds`) pushes tickets to kitchen screens in under 500ms with color-coded aging timers.
