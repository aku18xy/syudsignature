Key Capabilities for F&B SME Business Owners

Recipe-Linked Inventory (BOM)

Every coffee, burger, or pasta sold instantly depletes grams of ingredients from inventory with dynamic real-time COGS calculations.

Offline-First Resilience

Zero downtime when outlet Wi-Fi drops. Waiters can still punch orders, print kitchen tickets, and collect payments via IndexedDB.

Eliminate Cash Leakage

Blind shift closing, manager PIN overrides for voids and discounts, and automated End-of-Day Z-Reports with discrepancy alerts.
